RouteForge

A delivery route planner for small brands in Mumbai. Upload a CSV of stops and it finds the fastest order to drive them, using real road travel times from OpenStreetMap — then shows how much time you saved versus your original order.

Solar Power Predictor

Predicts how much power a solar panel will produce from the weather — sunlight, temperature, humidity and cloud cover. Built with classical regression models (no neural nets) and a Streamlit dashboard to compare them.

NSE Paper-Trading Bot

An automated bot for Indian stocks that trades with paper money only. I tested momentum strategies and whether news filings predict price moves. Biggest lesson: most strategies that look great in backtests fall apart on new data — so it stays on paper.

  • pandas
